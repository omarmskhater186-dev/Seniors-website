About the doctor

A specialist who treats your parent as a whole person

Professor Mohamed Shawky Khater is a geriatrician — a doctor who specialises in the medicine of later life. Older patients are rarely about a single diagnosis: it is the way several conditions, many medicines, memory, mood and mobility interact that decides how well someone actually lives day to day. That is exactly the picture geriatric medicine is built to see.

His approach is unhurried. Appointments leave room to listen — to the patient and to the family who knows them best — to explain things in plain language, and to agree a realistic plan rather than simply adding another prescription. The aim is steadier health, fewer crises and hospital trips, and more good days at home.

What we help with

Care explained in plain language

Here is what each part of our care actually means for your parent — no medical jargon.

Home visits

When leaving the house is hard or exhausting, the doctor comes to your parent — a full assessment in the comfort of their own home.

Complex & multiple-condition care

When there are several conditions at once — heart, diabetes, blood pressure, joints — we look at how they affect each other, so nothing is treated in isolation.

Medication review

A long list of pills can do more harm than good. We check everything your parent takes and safely simplify it — removing what is unnecessary or clashing.

Memory & dementia concerns

If you have noticed forgetfulness, confusion or changes in behaviour, we assess what is happening, explain it clearly, and guide the whole family on what comes next.

Falls & unsteadiness

Falls are not just “old age”. We find the real reasons behind them — balance, blood pressure, eyesight, medicines — and put practical steps in place to prevent the next one.

Full geriatric assessment

A thorough, head-to-toe review of health, mobility, memory, mood and medicines — bringing the whole picture together into one clear plan you can follow.

Care that comes to you

Home visits across Greater Cairo

For many older patients, the journey to a clinic is the hardest part of the day — and for families it means time off work, transport and a great deal of stress. So the doctor can come to your parent instead.

A home visit is a complete consultation, not a quick check. Seeing your parent in their own surroundings often tells us more than any clinic could — about safety, mobility and how they really manage day to day.

  • A full assessment and examination at home
  • Review of every medicine in the cabinet, together
  • Practical safety advice to help prevent falls
  • A clear written plan the family can follow
